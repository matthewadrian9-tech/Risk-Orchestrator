MANDATE

US long/short, industrials and insurance. Gross to 200%, net -20% to +40%.

THESIS

Property and casualty pricing has broken out of a fifteen-year soft market and
the equity market is still treating it as the top of a normal cycle. Rate has
run ahead of loss cost trend for eight consecutive quarters, reserve development
has been favourable rather than adverse, and the capital that historically
floods in to compete the rate away has not arrived at the scale it used to,
because the reinsurance side is now demanding returns it did not demand before.
Alongside that, the maintenance side of industrial demand is being mistaken for
the capital spending side. Repair, replacement and consumables spending tracks
the installed base and the age of it, not the order book for new projects, and
it holds up in periods when new construction does not. That distinction is the
whole position. This is not a bet on a capital spending recovery and it does not
need one.

EXPRESSION

Long the underwriters where the rate increases are already earned into the book:
MKL and PGR. Long the maintenance and consumables side: FAST and GWW, where the
revenue arrives against installed equipment rather than against new builds, and
APD on contracts that are effectively take-or-pay regardless of volume. LMT is
held for the backlog's annuity characteristics and its cash return, not for any
view about the appropriation cycle.

Shorts are funding positions rather than a thesis, chosen for low realised beta
and to keep the book near neutral: NEM, CHD, HRL, SJM, K and GIS. I have no
particular view on any of them and would swap them for anything with the same
characteristics.

CATALYSTS

Insurance renewal pricing at the January reset. Reserve development disclosure
with fourth-quarter results in February. Monthly industrial distribution
same-day sales.

RISKS

One soft catastrophe year and insurance rate rolls over immediately. If
maintenance spending turns out to track capital spending more closely than I
think, the long book loses its independence from the cycle and I have no second
argument.
