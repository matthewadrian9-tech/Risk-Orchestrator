MANDATE

US long/short, consumer and financials. Gross to 200%, net -20% to +40%, no
single name above 8% of NAV. No credit instruments, no listed options without
desk approval.

THESIS

The easing cycle has started and the cyclical half of the market is still
priced for a recession that did not arrive. Two years of restrictive policy
pushed capital into anything with a stable coupon, and the businesses whose
earnings actually move with the cycle were left on multiples that assume the
downturn is still coming. It does not need to arrive for this to work. It only
needs to keep not arriving. As the front end falls, funding costs drop against
loan books that reprice slowly, deferred equipment spending gets released, and
discretionary spend returns to the categories with the most operating leverage.
What has to be true is that cuts are sustained rather than one-and-done, and
that unemployment stays below roughly 4.8%. Above that the consumer credit leg
inverts and the whole book goes with it.

EXPRESSION

Long the highest-beta expression of that I can hold responsibly. DFS on card
receivables where reserve ratios were set for a recession nobody got. KEY as
the regional bank with the cleanest deposit repricing story into cuts. URI
where non-residential construction and deferred fleet replacement both bite.
MGM and UAL for discretionary recovery, FCX as the copper leg on an industrial
restart.

The short book funds it with the other side of the same trade — ABBV, NOC,
LNT, KO, DUK, PEP — bond substitutes bought for their coupon rather than their
numbers.

CATALYSTS

FOMC in December and again in March. Card issuer results in late January, where
reserve releases would first appear. Monthly master trust delinquency data.

RISKS

Delinquency formation reaccelerating two months running breaks this. If
unemployment prints above 4.8% before the reserve releases start, the longs are
value traps and I cut rather than average down.
