MANDATE

US long/short, financials and industrials. Gross to 200%, net -20% to +40%.

THESIS

Everyone can see what happens when the curve normalises. The disagreement worth
having is not about direction, it is about which vehicles still pay you for
being right. My view on the macro is not original and I will not pretend
otherwise: two years of tight money left the earnings-sensitive part of the
market discounting a contraction that has not shown up in any hard data, and a
lower front end unlocks lending and project spending simultaneously. Where I
differ from the consensus expression is that the household-facing vehicles have
already absorbed most of the re-rating. Positioning surveys put the obvious
consumer names in the top decile of crowding. Buying them now means paying for
a view I can get elsewhere at a discount, and it means owning the exit risk when
that crowd changes its mind at the same moment.

EXPRESSION

Same underlying driver, second-order vehicles. CFG and FITB are the deposit
franchises whose liability costs fall faster than their asset yields, which is
the identical mechanism the card names are owned for without the unsecured
consumer exposure attached. COF is the hedge between the two and is valued as
though its card book is the entire enterprise. HAL captures the completion
activity operators deferred while capital was expensive. LRCX is the wafer
fabrication equipment expression of the same unlocked spending, and BA the
aerospace one, where the delivery ramp is constrained by customer financing
before it is constrained by the factory.

Shorts fund it: LLY, CMS, MKC, DG, HSY, WEC. Assets whose valuation came from
the discount rate rather than from anything they did.

CATALYSTS

FOMC in December and March. Regional bank results mid-January, where net
interest margin guidance is the number. North American rig count, weekly.

RISKS

Correlation across this book is far higher than the sector spread implies, and I
size it accordingly rather than pretending otherwise. A hawkish surprise takes
every position down together. My out is the December meeting: no cut and no
guidance toward one, and I take the gross down by half before January.
