MANDATE

US long/short, sector agnostic with an industrial and financial tilt. Gross
capped at 200%, net -20% to +40%. Single name limit 10% of NAV.

THESIS

Rate cuts into an economy that never broke is the most underpriced setup on the
board. The sequence from here is mechanical rather than speculative: the front
end falls, financing costs drop, projects that were shelved at 5.5% get
released, consumer credit normalises against reserves built for a downturn that
did not happen, and earnings revisions follow with a lag the market
underestimates every single cycle. The consensus objection is that the cuts are
already discounted. They are discounted in the rates market. They are not
discounted in the equities that carry the earnings leverage, which still trade
where they traded when a hard landing was the base case.

EXPRESSION

Concentrated in the names where the operating leverage is largest. DFS and SYF
on the card side, where the reserve ratio is the whole argument. KEY for
deposit costs falling faster than asset yields. URI on deferred equipment
replacement. AMP for fee leverage on rising asset values. MGM for discretionary
spend at the point of highest incremental margin.

Shorts are the mirror image — MO, XEL, EA, BDX, REGN, PFE — assets bought as
bond substitutes through the tightening, whose multiples were set by the
discount rate rather than by anything in the business.

CATALYSTS

FOMC in December and March. Fourth-quarter industrial results in late January.
Non-residential construction spending, monthly.

RISKS

This is one bet on the cycle held six ways and I am not pretending otherwise.
The sector labels are cosmetic. If the labour market breaks, every long falls
together and the short book will not offset enough. Two consecutive negative
payroll prints and I halve the gross.
