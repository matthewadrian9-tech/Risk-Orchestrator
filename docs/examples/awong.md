MANDATE

US long/short, healthcare and services. Gross to 180%, net -20% to +40%.

THESIS

Value in healthcare is migrating out of the businesses that discover molecules
and into the businesses that measure, price and administer them, and the market
is still valuing both layers on the old split. A branded manufacturer facing a
patent cliff has a terminal value problem no pipeline slide fixes: reimbursement
pressure and PBM spread compression have taken pricing away permanently, and the
multiples still reflect a margin structure that will not return. The layer
underneath — diagnostics, instrumentation, benefit management, the exchanges that
clear the risk — gets paid per test, per member, per transaction, and that
revenue does not expire on a patent date. This is a structural view about where
margin sits in the chain. It does not depend on the rate path and I am not
positioned for one.

EXPRESSION

Long the measurement and administration layer. LH on testing volume, A on
instrumentation attach where the installed base does the work, ELV on medical
cost management, ICE for fee revenue on cleared volume rather than on direction.
MDLZ and T are income positions held for the coupon and are not part of this
argument.

Short the manufacturers on the wrong side of the same change: BMY and GILD into
patent cliffs with no replacement, MRK on reimbursement exposure, JNJ and PG
where the multiple was set in a period of pricing power that has gone, AEP as a
funding short.

CATALYSTS

CMS reimbursement guidance in the fourth quarter. PBM contract renewals through
the first quarter. Fourth-quarter results across the short book in February,
where gross margin is the only line I care about.

RISKS

A single large approval in the short book removes the patent cliff argument for
that name. If diagnostic volumes soften the long side loses its own story
rather than losing to the shorts.
