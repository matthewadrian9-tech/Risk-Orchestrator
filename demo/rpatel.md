MANDATE

US financials long/short. Gross to 200%, net -20% to +40%.

THESIS

Regional banks have not yet recognised the losses in their commercial real estate books, and the ones with the largest office concentration are the furthest behind. Reserve builds have run well below the level implied by appraisal marks on maturing loans, and extend-and-pretend only works while rates make refinancing plausible. The large money-centre banks have already reserved, have deposit franchises that funded through the last stress, and trade at a discount to the regionals on tangible book that makes no sense given the difference in asset quality.

EXPRESSION

Long JPM, BAC, WFC and USB. Short the regionals with the heaviest office exposure relative to tangible common equity - ZION, CMA and VLY.

CATALYSTS

Q3 results across the group in mid-October, where reserve builds are disclosed. Shared National Credit review results in the fourth quarter. Office loan maturities concentrated in the first half of next year.

RISKS

A rapid rate cut cycle would let borrowers refinance and defer the recognition indefinitely. If reserve builds at the regionals come in materially above expectation in one quarter, the gap closes at once and the short side is wrong.
