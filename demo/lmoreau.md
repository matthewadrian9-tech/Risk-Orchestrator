MANDATE

Consumer discretionary and staples long/short, North America. Gross to 200%, net -20% to +40%.

THESIS

Real wage erosion since 2021 has not reversed for the bottom two income quintiles, and the trading-down behaviour that began as a response to inflation has become habit. Off-price and deep discount formats are taking share permanently rather than cyclically, which the market keeps mistaking for a late-cycle defensive rotation that will unwind. It will not unwind, because the consumer who moved does not move back when prices stabilise - they move back when income recovers, and income has not.

EXPRESSION

Long the discount and off-price formats - DG, BJ, TJX, ROST. Short aspirational discretionary where the customer is exactly the one who left and where inventory has been building for three quarters: LULU, RH and W.

CATALYSTS

Q3 retail results through mid-November. Holiday sales data in early January. Monthly same-store sales from the discounters through the period.

RISKS

A genuine real-wage recovery would reverse the trade, and the payroll data would show it before the retail numbers do. If the discounters' comparable sales decelerate while the aspirational names re-accelerate, the thesis is broken.
