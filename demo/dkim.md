MANDATE

Technology hardware and industrials, long/short, gross capped at 200%, net -20% to +40%. Single position limit 10% of NAV.

THESIS

Power is the bottleneck. Everyone has worked out that AI needs compute; far fewer have worked out that compute needs cooling, switchgear and grid connection, and that those three things are supply-constrained in a way semiconductors are not. Fabs can be built in two years. A transformer factory takes longer and nobody wants to build one on the expectation of demand that might be a bubble. That asymmetry is why the pricing power sits with the equipment vendors and stays there. My view is that this holds for at least six more quarters and that the equity market is still modelling it as a cyclical peak.

EXPRESSION

The book is long VRT and ETN for thermal and electrical, SMCI where the rack-level backlog is visible, and MOD as the smaller thermal name the market has not repriced. Shorts are packaged food and household products - businesses with no volume growth trading at multiples that only made sense when rates were at zero.

CATALYSTS

Hyperscaler Q3 results in the first week of November. VRT and ETN report late October. A large utility capital plan update is expected in December.

RISKS

If order intake decelerates at either VRT or ETN the thesis is wrong and I will cut rather than average down. The specific number I am watching is organic orders growth below 15% year on year.
