MANDATE

Electrical equipment and utilities long/short, Europe and North America. Gross to 200%, net -20% to +40%.

THESIS

The binding constraint on AI compute is not silicon, it is the power equipment that gets electricity into the building. Hyperscaler capex has roughly doubled in two years and every dollar of it eventually needs transformers, switchgear, turbines and cooling that nobody built capacity for during a decade of flat utility spending. Lead times have gone from months to years and the vendors are setting price for the first time in twenty years. The market is still valuing this equipment layer on a normal industrial cycle, which assumes mean reversion that cannot happen while the backlog is contracted out past 2027. What has to be true is that hyperscaler capex guidance holds through the next two reporting seasons.

EXPRESSION

I am long the same shortage as everyone else but not through the same names. GEV for turbines and grid solutions, NVT and HUBB for electrical connection and enclosure, AOS where the commercial re-rating is masked by a boring water-heating business. The obvious datacenter equipment names already price the cycle and trade with semiconductor sentiment rather than with orders; these do not, yet, and they sell into the identical demand.

Shorts are packaged food - no volume growth, multiples set when rates were zero, the cleanest funding source in my universe.

CATALYSTS

Hyperscaler Q3 results in the first week of November are the number that matters. Utility capital plan updates through late October. FERC transmission ruling in December.

RISKS

A single hyperscaler cutting capex guidance takes this entire complex down at once, and my names will not be spared for being less obvious. If order intake at the large electrical vendors decelerates below 15% year on year the thesis is impaired.
